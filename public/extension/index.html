Edge Guard for Chrome

Stops AI agents, and web pages, from paying, sending, deleting or sharing without your OK.

  • Warns about hidden traps. Finds text you can't see that is written to steer an AI agent, and shows it to you.
  • Asks before anything risky. Payments, messages, deletions and sharing wait for your OK, in a window outside the page that the page's own code can't click.
  • Private. Page content never leaves your computer.

Install it in about a minute

Edge Guard is not in the Chrome Web Store yet, so Chrome needs four small steps instead of one click. You only do this once.

  1. Unzip the download

    Windows: right-click edge-guard-extension.zip in your Downloads, choose Extract All, then Extract.
    Mac: double-click the file. You get a folder called edge-guard-extension.
    Keep that folder: Chrome runs Edge Guard from it, so don't delete it afterwards.

  2. Open Chrome's extensions page

    Copy this address, paste it into Chrome's address bar and press Enter. (Chrome doesn't let websites link to it directly.)

    chrome://extensions

  3. Turn on Developer mode

    It's a switch in the top-right corner of that page. It only lets Chrome add extensions from a folder; you can turn it off again afterwards and Edge Guard keeps working.

  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder

    The button appears top-left. Choose the edge-guard-extension folder from step 1. Edge Guard appears in the list with a purple shield.

Then: click the puzzle piece next to the address bar and pin Edge Guard. Open it once: if your computer can run Google's on-device AI (Gemini Nano), click Download Gemini Nano so checks run privately. Without it, Edge Guard still pauses risky actions and asks you.

Try it safely

Open our pretend travel shop. It hides three instructions aimed at AI agents, and its buttons do nothing real. Edge Guard should warn you within a few seconds; then try Pay, Delete saved card and Sign in.

Good to know

  • What it can see: button labels and page text, on your computer. What you type into a form is only compared, in memory, to make sure it did not change while you decided; it is never stored or sent. Privacy policy.
  • Updates: download again and repeat step 4 with the new folder; or click the circular arrow on Edge Guard in chrome://extensions after replacing the folder.
  • To remove it: chrome://extensions, then Remove on Edge Guard.
