Edge Guard for AI browser agents: privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. Applies to the Chrome extension "Edge Guard for AI browser agents".

In short

Edge Guard reads web pages on your computer to pause risky actions and to find hidden instructions aimed at AI. Page content never leaves your computer. To judge a message or a deletion, it may send a short description of that one click to Google's Gemini. It does not sell, share or keep your data anywhere else.

What Edge Guard reads, on your computer

  • The text of the pages you visit, to find hidden instructions aimed at AI agents. This is checked by rules and, when your computer supports it, by Gemini Nano, an AI model that runs on your computer. It is never sent anywhere.
  • The labels of a button you or an AI agent clicks (its text, and the labels screen readers and AI agents read, for example "Pay $1,240"), the website's name (and, for a click inside an embedded frame, the name of the site around it) and the page title, when the click is a payment, message, deletion, sharing change or password submission.
  • Whether a form has a card-number field, whether a password field has been filled in, and the names and labels of the fields next to a button (for example "Amount" or "IBAN"), to tell a money transfer from an ordinary form.
  • When Edge Guard pauses a click, it notes what the form would send and compares it again just before sending, so a form that changed while you decided (another amount, another payee) is not sent on your approval. That comparison happens in memory on your computer; what you typed is never stored, logged or sent anywhere.

What may leave your computer

Only for messages and deletions, and only when the on-device AI is unavailable or to double-check its decision to let one through, Edge Guard sends this to Google's Gemini through Google Firebase: the button's label, the type of action, the website names and the page title. Nothing else. Payments, sharing changes, passwords and page content are never sent. Google processes that request under the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms.

What Edge Guard stores

  • On your computer only: whether Edge Guard is on, your last 50 decisions (site, button label, allowed or blocked, and why), and the scan result of each open tab until you close it.
  • You can clear all of it by removing the extension.

What Edge Guard does not do

  • It does not sell or share your data, show ads, or track you across sites.
  • It does not use your data for anything other than pausing risky actions and warning about hidden instructions.
  • It does not create an account or know who you are.
